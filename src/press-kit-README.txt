ABOUT
Caddy is a macOS productivity app that turns the left, right, and top edges of a Mac screen into small workstations. Each Caddy holds one body of work and its documents, notes, and reading panes.

PLATFORM
macOS 15 or later. Universal for Apple silicon and Intel.

PRIVACY
No account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party network service. Caddy reads only files the user chooses and stores its own state inside the App Sandbox.

CURRENT FEATURES SHOWN
- A flat, labeled Library above one or more reading panes.
- Multiple saved RTF notes that open in a reading pane, drag as files, and reveal in Finder.
- Notes, Library, and reader kept together after adding a file while writing.
- PDF and Word zoom using controls, keyboard shortcuts, and trackpad pinch.
- A Caddy-tab menu bar logo and a quiet lower-left Quick Add control.
- Simple General and per-Caddy settings.
