Finding Caddy

Caddy lives in the menu bar under its monochrome Caddy logo, and on the edges of your screen as colored tabs. There is no conventional main window and no Dock icon. If you have just installed it and cannot see anything, look at the right edge of your main display, the small + tab low on the left edge, and the menu bar. Click a colored tab to open a Caddy, and click it again to close the drawer.

The first launch normally opens a short introduction that ends with a Sample Caddy on the right edge and four bundled example documents. With Reduce Motion enabled, Caddy opens directly to the Sample Caddy.

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal, so it runs natively on Apple silicon and on Intel Macs.
