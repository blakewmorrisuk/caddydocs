Finding Caddy

Caddy lives in the menu bar as a small flag, and on the edges of your screen as colored tabs. There is no window and no Dock icon. That is the whole app. If you have just installed it and cannot see anything, look at the right edge of your main display, and at the menu bar. Click a tab to open a Caddy, and click it again to put it away.

The first launch opens a Sample Caddy on the right edge with four bundled example documents, so there is something to look at straight away.

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal, so it runs natively on Apple silicon and on Intel Macs.
