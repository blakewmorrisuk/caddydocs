Privacy Policy

Caddy Docs for macOS · Effective August 22, 2026

Caddy Docs (“Caddy”) is a macOS app made by Blake Morris. This policy is short because the app does very little with your information.

What Caddy collects

Nothing. Caddy has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. It opens no network connections of its own. No information about you, your Mac, or your documents leaves your computer through Caddy, so there is nothing for anyone to use, sell, or share.

What Caddy stores on your Mac

Caddy keeps references to the documents and folders you add, the notes you write, and your settings inside its own sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/com.caddydocs.Caddy. It reads your documents where they already live, and never copies or changes them.

That container is everything Caddy retains, for as long as you keep the app. To remove all of it, drag Caddy to the Trash and then delete that folder. Your own documents are untouched.

Third parties

Caddy includes no third-party code or services and shares nothing with anyone. Open in Word, Open in Preview, and Reveal in Finder hand a document to the app that owns it, at your instruction, and that app governs what happens next. If a document in one of your Caddies lives only in the cloud, as OneDrive, iCloud Drive and Dropbox files often do, then opening or previewing it asks the provider you already run to download it to your Mac first. Caddy opens no connection of its own, and that provider’s own policy governs what it does. This website is served as static files with no analytics, no cookies, no trackers, and no third-party requests of any kind.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
